## Context

A personal project born from a question every game night asks: what do we play without overthinking it? The site
answers in one key press: give the number of players and it suggests free online games that fit, which you can filter
on several criteria.

![The What we play? home on a computer: “À quoi on joue à 6 ?” in a bubble, the keypad of player counts, an old Mac with a TV face and six coloured pawns, then the search and the filters.](./home.webp 'The home, for a game night with six.')

## What I did

Everything, from the mockup to going live:

- the art direction;
- the web development;
- the pure-CSS 3D;
- choosing the sites listed.

![Three phone screens: choosing the number of players, then the TV suggesting Le Petit Bac, then Lyrix after a new draw.](./phone-count.webp 'On a phone: the number of players, then a game drawn at random, to draw again.') ![](./phone-pick.webp) ![](./phone-reroll.webp)

## Technical challenges

A site that is pleasant to use, fast on every device, optimised for search engines and AI answers (SEO and GEO), and
multilingual.

![The page of games to play alone, in the light theme: “À quoi je joue en solo ?”, the filters, then seven games as coloured cards.](./solo-light.webp 'One page per player count and per language, here solo and in the light theme.')

## Results

- More than 30 hand-picked games, with regular additions.
- 2D and 3D displays optimised for every device.

![A grid of colourful game cards: Wikipedia Speedruns, Le Petit Bac, Make it Meme, Codenames, skribbl.io and more, each with its category and player count.](./games.webp 'Part of the selection: twenty games for four players.')
