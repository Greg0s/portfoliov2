A school project for four, with Emma Veauvy, Juliette Jeannin and Lucie Augier, about combinatorial literature, in the
spirit of Queneau’s _Hundred Thousand Billion Poems_: an exhibition, a site that composes sonnets, haikus, tales and
stories on demand, and a video to promote it.
