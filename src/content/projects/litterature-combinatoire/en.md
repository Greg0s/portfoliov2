Combinatorial literature is the creation of texts by randomly combining other texts. It was the subject of my engineering final-year project, within a group of four. The result: an exhibition, a promotional video and a site to discover different forms of combinatorial literature (generated sonnets, haikus, tales and choose-your-own-adventure stories).
