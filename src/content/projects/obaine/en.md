## Context

Obaine is a platform where fans support athletes financially, in exchange for exclusive content and experiences. As
the company was launching, I built its entire front end from the team’s designs, for a public opening on a set date.

## What I did

The platform’s entire front end, in close collaboration with a UI designer and a back-end developer:

- the home, actions and all-athletes pages;
- the mobile version of the site;
- the API communication and a shared state across the app.

All in Vue 3 with Pinia and Vue Router, tested with Vitest and shipped in Docker.

## Technical challenges

A working MVP in record time, with a strict deadline and a lot of autonomy.

## Results

- The MVP fully built and working in 2 months.
- More than 100 customers on the platform.
